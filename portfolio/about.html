About Me

Stanie Maba | Software & Platform Engineer

My engineering journey began with Linux system administration, where I developed a strong foundation in operating systems, troubleshooting, configuration, security, and production support. I later transitioned into DevOps and infrastructure engineering, working with cloud environments, CI/CD pipelines, automation, containers, and modern application deployment practices.

As a Platform Engineer, I have worked closely with development teams and supported applications throughout their delivery lifecycle. Exposure to software development projects increased my interest in understanding not only how applications are deployed and operated, but also how they are designed and built from the frontend through the backend.

I am now deliberately developing my Full Stack Engineering skills. My goal is to combine software development with my existing infrastructure knowledge so that I can understand the complete lifecycle of an application—from writing the code and integrating APIs to containerizing, deploying, monitoring, troubleshooting, and supporting it in production.
